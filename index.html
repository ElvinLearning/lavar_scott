01 — The Story

Built in a
family garage.

Racing runs in the blood. His grandfather Wayne Sr. opened the family auto shop in 1978 and drag raced with his friends. His mother Sonia held an NHRA license and ran drag strips across PA, NJ and Delaware. When Lavar was four, his grandfather built him a go-kart — and pushed him around the track before he was even old enough for an engine.

From quarter midgets at Airport Speedway to a 600 Micro Sprint at 12, Lavar earned every lap. The 2019 NASCAR Driver Development Combine put him in a Legend Car with Rev Racing; five seasons later he left ARCA with Rookie of the Year in 2024 and a runner-up finish in the drivers championship.

He debuted in the NASCAR O'Reilly Auto Parts Series in 2025 with Alpha Prime — qualifying 22nd and finishing 19th at both Dover and World Wide Technology Raceway. Now he's full-time in the No. 45, one of only three Black drivers at NASCAR's national level, carrying his family, his hometown, and every kid who never saw themselves in this sport.

That last part is the business case. Lavar doesn't just drive the car — he builds the audience around it: nearly 100,000 followers, a training and lifestyle channel that runs 52 weeks a year, and community work that puts brands in front of people long after the checkered flag.

03 — Off The Track

The work nobody films.

A stock car cockpit runs past 120°F. A race is three-plus hours of sustained load, heart rate pinned, hands and neck taking every bump. Lavar trains for it year-round — and that training is a content channel of its own, running the 19 weeks a year when there's no race to post about.

Format 01

Weight room

Lift days, neck and grip work, the unglamorous conditioning that keeps a rookie sharp at lap 180. Apparel, equipment and supplement partners live here natively.

Format 02

Fuel & recovery

What he eats on a Thursday travel day, hydration through a hot race, sleep and recovery between back-to-backs. Straight-line fit for nutrition and beverage brands.

Format 03

Race-week build

A Monday-to-Sunday series that turns one race into seven days of content — sim work, travel, hauler, garage, grid. Partners get seven touchpoints, not one.

Format 04

Fit for the job

Young, disciplined, drug-free, on camera in the gym as often as the car. A brand-safe athlete story that translates outside motorsport audiences.

04 — On The Ground

More than a
race car.

The No. 45 already carries the Urban Affairs Coalition — one of the Philadelphia region's biggest workforce-development organizations. That partnership is the template: motorsport as a doorway into careers most kids never hear about.

A NASCAR team is a workforce — fabricators, engine builders, mechanics, engineers, logistics, media. Lavar uses the platform to show that, in front of the students and communities that rarely get a look inside the garage.

For a partner, that's real local-market goodwill with photography, video and earned media attached — the part of a sponsorship that keeps working after the season ends.
