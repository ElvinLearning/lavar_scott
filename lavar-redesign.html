01 — The Story

Built in a
family garage.

Racing runs in the blood. His grandfather Wayne Sr. opened the family auto shop in 1978 and drag raced with his friends. His mother Sonia held an NHRA license and ran drag strips across PA, NJ and Delaware. When Lavar was four, his grandfather built him a go-kart — and pushed him around the track before he was even old enough for an engine.

From quarter midgets at Airport Speedway to years of grinding on Northeast dirt, Lavar earned every lap. A first late model win at Hickory in 2021. Two full ARCA seasons with Rev Racing and NASCAR's Drive for Diversity program — stacking 22 top-five finishes.

Now, at 22, he's a rookie on NASCAR's second-biggest stage — one of only three Black drivers competing at the national level — carrying his family, his hometown, and every kid who never saw themselves in this sport.

"There was no Plan B." — Lavar Scott
