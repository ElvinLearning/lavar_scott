On The Track

Free TV.
Every weekend.

The NASCAR O'Reilly Auto Parts Series, the series formerly known as Xfinity, is where the sport's best talent races and where Cup Series names come from. Since 2025 it has had something most properties never get: a single, permanent, free broadcast home.

The home

The CW, through 2031

Exclusive network home of the series on a deal running through the 2031 season. Fans always know where to find it.

The access

Over the air, no paywall

Every race airs on free, over-the-air broadcast television. No cable package, no streaming subscription.

The volume

A full season, live

Live races all season, plus practice and qualifying every weekend, all on a national broadcast.

The Team

Alpha Prime Racing.

Craig Martins founded Martins Motorsports in 2014 so his son Tommy Joe could prove himself at the top level. Caesar Bacarella, an entrepreneur and sports car racer, turned from driving to building an operation of his own. The two met racing in 2017, became friends, and when Craig stepped away they joined forces. Alpha Prime Racing went from a single-car family team to a multi-car organization with two full-time entries.

The owners

Drivers, not suits

Tommy Joe Martins co-owns the team and spots for Lavar himself. Decisions get made by people who have been strapped into the car.

The position

More with less

One of the smaller operations in the garage, competing against teams that spend far more, and beating them often enough to prove the point.

The fit

A budget that matters here

At a superteam a mid six-figure deal is a rounding error. Here it is material, and it is treated that way: access, attention and reporting included.
