On The Ground

More than a
race car.

In June 2026 Alpha Prime Racing and the Urban Affairs Coalition launched the Alpha Prime Racing Workforce Development Initiative with UAC's Team Racing-2-Education, a national platform creating real pathways into motorsports. Lavar's No. 45 runs as its visible ambassador all season.

A NASCAR team is a workforce. The program puts students and young adults inside race team operations: engineering, mechanics, data analytics, automotive technology, logistics, marketing, content production, business operations and event management, through education, mentorship and hands-on experience across the 2026 season.

Lavar's own route ran from the Philadelphia area to the national stage, which is the journey the program exists to make repeatable.
