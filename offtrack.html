Off The Track

The work nobody films.

A stock car cockpit gets past 120 degrees. A race is three hours of that, heart rate pinned, hands and neck absorbing every bump in the track. None of it looks hard on TV. Lavar trains for it all year, in season and out.

Format 01

Weight room

Lift days, neck work, grip work. None of it is interesting to watch, and all of it is why he is still sharp on lap 180.

Format 02

Fuel and recovery

What he eats on a travel day, how he stays hydrated through a hot race, and how he recovers before doing it again next weekend.

Format 03

Race-week build

One race takes a whole week. Sim work early, travel midweek, then hauler, garage and grid.

Format 04

Fit for the job

He is in the gym about as often as he is in the car. That is an athlete's routine, not a driver's schedule.

A driver's audience watches on Saturday.
