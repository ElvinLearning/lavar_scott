Off The Track

The work nobody films.

A stock car cockpit runs past 120 degrees Fahrenheit. A race is three-plus hours of sustained load, heart rate pinned, hands and neck taking every bump. Lavar trains for it year-round, between race weekends and through the off-season.

Format 01

Weight room

Lift days, neck and grip work: the unglamorous conditioning that keeps a rookie sharp deep into a run.

Format 02

Fuel and recovery

What he eats on a travel day, hydration through a hot race, sleep and recovery between back-to-backs.

Format 03

Race-week build

A Monday-to-Sunday routine that turns one race into a full week: sim work, travel, hauler, garage, grid.

Format 04

Fit for the job

Young, disciplined, and in the gym as often as the car. An athlete's routine, not just a driver's schedule.
